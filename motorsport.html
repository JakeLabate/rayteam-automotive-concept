RayTeam Motorsport

The race team of RayTeam Automotive. Its first race was the 2024 Pikes Peak International Hill Climb, in an Audi Group B tribute car built and driven by Laszlo Keskeny.

Pikes Peak 2024 result

12:57.142

Official time at the 102nd Pikes Peak International Hill Climb on June 23, 2024. Car number 10, Pikes Peak Open division, 14th in class.

The course climbs 4,725 feet at an average grade of about 7 percent.

Race record

Two seasons in, the team has gone from one car to two.

  1. June 23, 2024

    102nd Pikes Peak International Hill Climb, Colorado

    The team's first race. Laszlo Keskeny, a Pikes Peak rookie, drove a 1986 Audi 4000 built as a Group B Audi Sport Quattro tribute to the summit in 12:57.142, 14th in the Pikes Peak Open division.

  2. September 7, 2024

    Mt. Ascutney Hillclimb, Vermont

    Episode 1 of the team's hill climb campaign series.

  3. October 20, 2024

    Okemo Hillclimb, Vermont

    Episode 2 of the campaign series.

  4. June 2025

    103rd Pikes Peak International Hill Climb, Colorado

    The second campaign grew from one Group B tribute car to two. Rookie Alex Kim entered an Audi S1 E2 tribute with the team, as RACER reported ahead of the race.

Films from the campaign

Each video loads from YouTube when you press play. The channel also covers the Burke Mountain Hillclimb, ice racing, and the 2026 Climb to the Clouds.

The cars

1986 Audi S1 E2

The S1 E2 was Audi's Group B rally car: a turbocharged five-cylinder engine and all-wheel drive in a short, wide body with a large rear wing. The team's car is a tribute to it, and it is the shape that climbed Pikes Peak in 2024.

1987 Audi UR Quattro

The road car that started it. The original Quattro put all-wheel drive into a performance coupe and changed rallying.
