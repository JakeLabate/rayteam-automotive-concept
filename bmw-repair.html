What we see most on BMW

Approved repairs carry a 24-month, 24,000-mile warranty.

  • Valve cover and oil filter housing gasket leaks

    This is the most common fault on BMWs between five and ten years old. Engine heat hardens the rubber until it cracks. You will notice a burning-oil smell, smoke under the hood, spots on the garage floor, or a low-oil warning. The shop leak-tests to find the source, fits a new OEM gasket, and tops the oil back up.

  • Electric water pump and thermostat failure

    When either part fails, the engine overheats and the car may drop into limp mode. The fault is confirmed with BMW's factory diagnostic system and component testing. The repair drains the coolant, replaces the failed parts with OEM ones, then refills and bleeds the system.

  • Carbon buildup on intake valves

    Direct injection sprays fuel into the cylinder, so nothing washes the intake valves clean. Carbon builds up, airflow drops, and the engine loses efficiency. A borescope confirms it and walnut blasting removes it.
