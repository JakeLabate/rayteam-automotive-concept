What is the car doing?

Choose the closest match to see what it usually means and which service covers it.

A warning light is on

The car has stored a fault. A factory-level scan reads it, and a test plan confirms the cause before any part is replaced.

If the check engine light is flashing, the engine is misfiring. Ease off and book a diagnosis soon.

Check engine light diagnosis, or call (781) 209-1400.

It is overheating

Pull over safely and shut the engine off. Driving an overheated engine is how a cooling fault becomes an engine repair.

Then call the shop so a technician can inspect the cooling system and find what failed.

Engine repair, or call (781) 209-1400.

It is leaking

Oil usually comes from a hardened gasket: valve cover, oil filter housing, oil cooler, or rear main seal. The shop leak-tests to find the source and replaces the gasket with an OEM part.

A burning-oil smell or smoke under the hood means oil is reaching something hot. Do not leave that one.

Oil leak repair, or call (781) 209-1400.

It will not start

Start with the battery. On many German cars a new battery must be registered or coded to the car, and the shop does that as part of the replacement.

If the battery is healthy, the fault is traced through the wiring, sensors, and control modules.

Electrical repair, or call (781) 209-1400.

The brakes feel or sound wrong

Squealing, grinding, or a soft pedal each point somewhere different: pads, rotors, or fluid. RayTeam measures brake fluid for moisture and fits OEM and OE-specification German parts.

Brake and clutch service, or call (781) 209-1400.

It shifts roughly

Harsh, late, or slipping shifts call for a transmission diagnosis. On a Volkswagen or Audi with a DSG, the shop recommends a fluid, filter, and o-ring service every 40,000 miles.

Transmission repair, or call (781) 209-1400.

It idles rough or hesitates

On a direct-injection engine, hesitation on a cold start and an uneven idle often mean carbon on the intake valves. The fix is walnut blasting, usually due around 45,000 to 50,000 miles.

Carbon cleaning, or call (781) 209-1400.

The A/C or defrost is weak

Weak cooling in summer and glass that will not clear on a wet morning are the same fault. The shop checks for leaks and compressor problems.

A/C repair, or call (781) 209-1400.
